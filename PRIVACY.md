# Privacy Policy

**App:** Crypto Market Analysis (CmA)
**Provider:** Kruen s.r.o., K Hájku 1714, 509 01 Nová Paka, Czech Republic
**Last updated:** 17 September 2026

## Summary

The app **does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.** There are
no user accounts, no registration and no sign-in, and the app contains no
analytics, advertising or user-tracking tools.

## What data we process

None. The app never asks you for a name, e-mail address, phone number or any
other information, and it has no access to any. It does not access your
contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. The only Android permission
it requests is internet access.

## Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only:

- **a cache of downloaded market metrics**, so it does not re-download the
  same data on every refresh;
- **cryptocurrency icons** downloaded the first time they are displayed.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app
or clear its data.

## Internet connections

To display current data, the app connects to the following services:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `cma-data.kruen.workers.dev` | our own data gateway: precomputed metrics and coin profiles |
| `api.binance.com`, `data-api.binance.vision` | live prices, volumes and price history |
| `coin-images.coingecko.com` | cryptocurrency icons |
| `api.alternative.me` | the Fear & Greed market sentiment index |
| `api.coingecko.com`, `api.github.com`, `api.llama.fi` | fallback sources used only if the gateway cannot be reached |

As with ordinary web browsing, each such connection exposes your **IP address**
and technical connection details to the operator of that service. Those
operators process this data under their own privacy policies, not ours, and we
have no access to it.

The app sends them **no device identifier and nothing that could identify who
made the request.** All requests are anonymous and identical for every user.

Requests to our own gateway carry a fixed application key that is the same in
every copy of the app. It identifies the app, not you, and it is not linked to
any user. The gateway keeps no logs that would allow individual users to be
distinguished; it exists because the data providers' licence terms do not
permit the data to be redistributed publicly.

The gateway runs on the infrastructure of Cloudflare, Inc., which processes
connection data (including IP addresses) on our behalf as our hosting
provider, under its own
[privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer any data, because we hold none.
